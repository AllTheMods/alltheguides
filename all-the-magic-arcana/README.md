# All The Magic - Arcana

![](<../.gitbook/assets/arcana logo.png>)

**All The Magic** is back for its third installment with _**Arcana**_, the best assemblage of mods for 1.21.1 that are available for **NeoForge**, Powerful magic, monsters and wondrous locations await your discovery in this pack.

You have conquered **All The Mods**, but can you conquer _**All The Magic**_? With only limited technology to hold your hand see the world through different eyes as you rely on what the mystic arts have to offer.

With a complete rewrite to the **ATM star**’s recipe and each mod intertwined, explore the depths of mods such as Theurgy, Primal Magick or one of the few tech exceptions to fulfil your needs for storage Applied Energistics 2? But there is a catch, your only ‘ **source**’ of power is Ars Nouveau Source!
